Pet Lovers World · Account and data deletion

Request deletion of your account and data

You can request deletion of your Pet Lovers World account and associated personal data without needing to visit the Google Play Store.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to petsloversworld07@gmail.com with the subject Pet Lovers World – Account deletion request.
  2. State the email address associated with your Pet Lovers World account. You may also request deletion of specific personal data while keeping your account, if feasible.
  3. We may ask you to verify that you control the account before fulfilling a deletion request. Do not send your password or one-time verification codes.
  4. We will review your request and explain the steps needed, including whether any limited information must be retained under applicable law.

If an account-deletion feature is available inside your installed version of the app, you may also find it under Menu → Settings → Delete account and data. If it fails or is not available, use the email request method above.

What the request covers

Account details, profile information, pet profiles, uploaded photos and videos, posts, messages, and other personal data associated with your account, to the extent held by Pet Lovers World and permitted to be deleted under applicable law. You can also request deletion of particular data without deleting the entire account.

Retention and exceptions

Some limited information may need to be retained where necessary to comply with legal obligations, safeguard animal welfare, investigate abuse, or resolve disputes. If an exception applies, we will explain the categories involved and the applicable retention basis when responding to your request. Copies in backups may have a separate deletion cycle.
