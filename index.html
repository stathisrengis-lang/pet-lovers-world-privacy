Privacy Policy — Pet Lovers World

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Pet Lovers World is a platform connecting pet owners, animal lovers, volunteers, animal welfare organizations, and pet-related services.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features used, the application may process account details, email addresses, profile information, photos, posts, pet information, messages, location information, animal welfare reports, and technical diagnostic data.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide application features, manage accounts, display local animal reports, facilitate communication, support animal welfare activities, and maintain application security.

3. Location Information

Location information may be used to display nearby animals, reports, volunteers, and pet-related services. Users can manage location permissions through their device settings.

4. User-Generated Content

Information and media submitted as public posts may be visible to other users. Users should avoid publishing sensitive personal information.

5. Third-Party Services

Depending on the application's active configuration, services may include Supabase for backend infrastructure, Google Maps for mapping, and Google AdMob for advertising.

6. Security and Retention

We aim to apply appropriate measures to protect personal information. Specific retention and deletion procedures will be documented according to the application's verified production functionality.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may request access, correction, or deletion of their personal data by contacting us.

8. Children's Privacy

Age eligibility and protections for minors will be established consistently with the application's final audience settings and applicable laws.

9. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in application features, data practices, or legal requirements.
