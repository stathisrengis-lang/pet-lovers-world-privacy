Privacy Policy — Pet Lovers World

Last updated: 23 November 2026.

English · Ελληνικά · Account deletion / Διαγραφή λογαριασμού

1. Who we are

Pet Lovers World is an Android application for pet owners, volunteers, animal welfare groups and pet-related services. For privacy matters and requests, contact the app operator at petsloversworld07@gmail.com. This policy applies to Pet Lovers World and the features actually available in the installed version.

2. What data we process

  • Account and profile: email address, display name, account identifier, profile bio, selected area, profile photo and account preferences when provided.
  • Animal welfare content: pet details, animal reports, SOS or lost-and-found posts, photos, videos, comments, likes and other user-submitted content.
  • Messaging: in-app messages and related conversation information when users communicate.
  • Location: approximate location or an area chosen by the user to show nearby content and services. Users can control Android location permission. Public animal reports may show approximate map positions rather than a user's home address.
  • Technical and advertising data: device identifiers, diagnostics and usage information may be processed by the active Google Maps, Google Mobile Ads/AdMob and Firebase messaging components, as applicable to the specific app build and user choices.

3. Why we use data

To create and secure accounts, provide profiles and community features, display local posts and nearby animal welfare alerts, deliver messages and notifications, maintain performance, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. Where local sponsored posts or AdMob are enabled, approximate area and relevant technical identifiers may be used for advertising and measurement as permitted by applicable law and user consent or settings.

4. Who can see or receive data

Content intentionally submitted as a public post may be visible to other users; direct messages are intended for their participants. Infrastructure providers may process information to deliver the service. Depending on enabled features, these include Supabase (authentication, database and storage), Google (Maps SDK, Google Mobile Ads/AdMob and Firebase messaging) and other services disclosed in the app. Ad-related processing can involve Google's advertising partners under their terms and applicable consent choices. We do not claim that merely displaying a user-selected public post makes all its contents available to advertisers.

5. Choice, consent and permissions

Uploading media, posting content, sending messages and granting device location permission are user-initiated actions. A display name, region and an account authentication method may be required for online account functionality, while local preview availability may differ. Where required, permission or consent controls should be offered before non-essential advertising processing. Users may also change Android permissions from their device settings. Turning off permission may limit nearby features.

6. Storage, protection and retention

The service uses encrypted network connections and access controls such as database row-level security. No system can guarantee absolute security. Data may be kept while an account is active and for as long as needed to provide the requested features. After a verified deletion request, we work to delete the account and associated data, subject to limited retention needed for legal duties, fraud or abuse investigations, disputes, security and backup rotation. Where retention is required, we will explain the applicable data categories and grounds in response to the request. We do not promise immediate deletion of every backup copy.

7. Your rights and deletion requests

You can ask for access, correction or deletion of personal data and exercise other rights available under applicable data protection law. To request deletion of your account or selected data without deleting the account, use our deletion instructions or email petsloversworld07@gmail.com. We may verify account ownership without requesting your password. The in-app delete option may not be available or operational in every build; the email request route remains available.

8. Children

The current Google Play target-audience setting is adults aged 18 and over. Pet Lovers World is not directed to children; users who are under 18 should not create an account. If we learn that children's personal data was provided improperly, we will review a removal request.

9. Updates and contact

This policy may change if functionality, providers or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the current text. Contact petsloversworld07@gmail.com for privacy questions.
